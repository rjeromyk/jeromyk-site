Straight Answers

Questions Agency & Business Owners Ask

Do you only work with insurance agencies?

No. While insurance customer acquisition and agency scaling is my deepest vertical ($38M+ ad spend, 1.19M leads), I also provide hands-on Business Systems & Efficiency Consulting to growth-focused business owners across industries who need operational workflows, CRM data architecture, and unit economic financial models built to scale profitably.

What size agency do you work with?

I work best with IMOs, FMOs, and agency owners spending $50K+/month on lead generation — or those ready to scale to that level. The infrastructure I build is designed for high volume; below roughly $50K/month, it's usually more than you need yet.

Do you manage the ad spend, or just advise?

Both — it's done-for-you. I architect the system, build the funnels and automation, and hands-on manage the ad accounts. I'm currently managing $3M+/month in ad spend across clients. You're hiring an operator, not a consultant who hands you a slide deck.

Which ad platforms do you run?

Primarily Meta (Facebook and Instagram), where the majority of my $3M+/month runs, plus Google Ads. Every campaign runs inside Meta's financial-services compliance rules from day one.

How much does it cost to work with you?

It depends on scope — whether you need full lead-gen infrastructure, ad management, CRM and automation, or all of it. Rather than quote a generic number, I scope each engagement to your product lines and growth target on a strategy call. Book one here and I'll give you a clear picture.
