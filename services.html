FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only work with insurance agencies?

No. While insurance customer acquisition is my deepest vertical ($38M+ ad spend, 1.19M leads), I also provide Business Systems & Efficiency Consulting to growth-focused business owners across industries who need operational workflows, CRM architecture, and financial models built to scale profitably.

What size business or agency do you work with?

For agency ad management, I work best with IMOs, FMOs, and agency owners spending $50K+/month (or ready to scale to that level). For Business Systems Consulting, I work with established companies generating $500K+ in annual revenue that want to eliminate bottlenecks, automate operations, and maximize profit margins.

Do you manage the ad spend, or just advise?

Both. For ads management, it is 100% done-for-you: creative, media buying, tracking, and compliance. For systems consulting, I deliver architecture, implementation, and automation workflows so your team can execute friction-free.

Which ad platforms and technologies do you use?

For advertising: Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads. For systems & automation: GoHighLevel, Supabase, ViciDial/telecom integrations, custom webhook workflows, Zapier/Make, and modern AI automation tools.

How much does it cost to work with you?

It depends on scope — whether you need full customer acquisition infrastructure, done-for-you ad management, a business systems overhaul, or a complete growth partnership. Rather than quote a generic number, I scope each engagement to your goals on a free strategy call. Book one here and I'll give you a clear picture.
