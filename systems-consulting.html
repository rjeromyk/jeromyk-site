The Operational Ceiling

Why Growth Breaks Companies Without Architecture

When a business reaches $500K to $2M+ in revenue, what got you here will no longer get you there. Brute-force hustle and fragmented software turn into an operational bottleneck that burns out your staff and destroys profit margins.

⚠️

The Copy-Paste Software Trap

You're paying for 8 different SaaS tools — billing, forms, CRM, email marketing, scheduler, spreadsheets. Your team spends 15–25 hours per week manually transferring data between apps because none of them actually talk to each other cleanly.

Lead & Pipeline Leakage

Case Study · Telecom & Dialer Systems

Restructuring a 15-Person Sales Floor with High-Throughput Dialer Routing & 2.2x Active Talk Time

The Solution: Leveraging Jeromy's experience building TeleCRM and integrating ViciDial/Twilio VoIP architectures, we implemented an automated power-dialer queue with skill-based routing, instant call transfer logic, and automated dispositions. Rep talk time more than doubled within 3 weeks.
